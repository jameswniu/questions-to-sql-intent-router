# HO-2025 edition: percentage wind and hail deductible

To: Claims and underwriting staff, all regions
From: Personal Lines Underwriting
Date: November 15, 2024
Re: UW-25-01, HO-2025 edition: percentage wind and hail deductible

Form HO-2025 replaces HO-2023 for losses on or after January 1, 2025. The edition that applies is set by the date of loss, not the date the claim is reported, so a 2024 loss reported in 2025 is still handled under HO-2023.

In the West and South regions, the deductible for windstorm or hail becomes 2% of the Coverage A limit instead of a flat $1,000. On a home in Colorado at the state's median Coverage A of $414,000, the deductible for a wind or hail claim is now $8,280. On a home in Texas at the state's median Coverage A of $261,000, the deductible for a wind or hail claim is now $5,220. In the North and East regions, wind and hail keep the $1,000 deductible.

Two other terms change in every region. The fungi, mold and wet rot sublimit falls from $10,000 to $5,000, and a roof surface more than 15 years old on the date of loss is settled at actual cash value rather than replacement cost.

Expect fewer and larger wind and hail payments in the West and South regions from the spring of 2025, and more of those claims closed without payment because the damage falls below the deductible. Explain the deductible in dollars on first contact, so the insured is not surprised by the decision.
